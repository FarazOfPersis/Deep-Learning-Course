## 📚 Core Topics Covered

### 1. Foundations & Optimization

* **Neural Networks from Scratch:** Implementing forward passes, backpropagation, and manual weight updates to understand core mechanics.
* **Optimization Landscape:** Analyzing how different optimizers, including SGD, Adam, and RMSprop, adjust weights and learning rates.
* **Training Techniques:** Understanding loss functions, gradient-based optimization, regularization, and practical neural network training.

### 2. Convolutional & Recurrent Networks

* **Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs):** Building and training CNN architectures for image classification and other visual tasks.
* **Image Segmentation & OCR:** Leveraging dense prediction architectures to process, segment, and read visual sequences, including Captcha/OCR tasks.
* **Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs):** Managing hidden states and sequential dependencies in temporal data.
* **LSTMs:** Handling long-range dependencies with gated recurrent architectures.

### 3. Transformers & Large Language Models

* **Transformers:** Understanding and implementing attention mechanisms and the transformer architecture.
* **Large Language Models (LLMs):** Working with large pretrained language models and understanding how they are built and used.
* **Fine-Tuning & PEFT:** Adapting large foundational language models efficiently under strict hardware and VRAM limitations using parameter-efficient fine-tuning methods (e.g., LoRA).
* **Inference Optimization & RAG:** Making model inference faster and more memory-efficient, and connecting LLMs to vector search systems and databases to ground responses in external data.

### 4. Generative Models

* **Variational Autoencoders (VAEs):** Learning latent representations and generating data through probabilistic encoders and decoders, including VQ-VAE variants.
* **Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs):** Training generator and discriminator networks adversarially to synthesize realistic samples.
* **Diffusion Models:** Studying and implementing diffusion-based image generation through iterative denoising.

### 5. Self-Supervised Learning & Vision-Language Models

* **Self-Supervised Learning (SSL):** Learning useful representations from unlabeled data by designing pretext or proxy objectives instead of relying on manually labeled datasets.
* **Contrastive & Non-Contrastive Methods:** Implementing and comparing frameworks such as SimCLR, MoCo, BYOL, and DINO, covering augmentations, positive/negative pairs, projection heads, and the associated loss functions.
* **Representation Learning:** Studying how networks learn transferable features that can be reused for downstream tasks.
* **Vision-Language Models:** Exploring CLIP and multimodal representation learning through image-text alignment.
